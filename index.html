Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) Architecture

What is Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis?

Standard document-level sentiment analysis assigns a single overall sentiment label (Positive/Negative) to an entire piece of text. However, real customer reviews are nuanced and contain mixed feedback across different features or aspects.

Example: "The food was delicious, but the service was slow."
  • Traditional SA: Mixed / Neutral (loses detailed feedback)
  • ABSA:
    • Aspect: Food ➔ Positive (Opinion: "delicious")
    • Aspect: Service ➔ Negative (Opinion: "slow")

Engine Processing Pipeline

  1. Clause Segmentation: The raw text is decomposed into discrete clause units by splitting on conjunction shifters (but, however, although, yet) and sentence delimiters.
  2. Aspect Entity Recognition: Each clause is parsed against domain taxonomies (Restaurant, Hotel, Tech, E-Commerce, Custom) to locate target entity mentions.
  3. Negation & Modifier Shifting: The engine inspects 3-word context windows around opinion adjectives to apply negation flips ("not good" ➔ Negative) and intensity amplifiers ("extremely fast" ➔ Boost Score).
  4. Span Extraction & Highlight Mapping: Character offsets are calculated to enable real-time interactive text highlighting in the web UI.
